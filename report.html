UAV–UGV Cooperative Tracking — ugv_uav_control

A drone with a downward camera is the only sensor; it perceives a TurtleBot3 and steers it with observability-aware MPC, while planning its own trajectory online via Tube MPC.

1 · Overview

This package implements a cooperative aerial–ground guidance system in ROS 2 + Gazebo. A simulated x500 quadrotor carries a downward-facing camera and is the sole source of perception: it detects an ArUco marker (ID 55) on a TurtleBot3 burger and extracts the painted lane centerline from the same image. A 5-state Extended Kalman Filter fuses the ArUco pose with wheel odometry into a belief state $(\mu,\Sigma)$ for the ground robot. Two model-predictive controllers then close the loop: a belief-space MPC drives the UGV along the lane while actively keeping itself inside the drone's field of view (it penalizes the covariance trace), and a two-tier Tube MPC plans and robustly tracks the UAV's own 3-D trajectory so the camera keeps the target in frame.

The ground robot has no onboard sensing of its own pose — everything it knows about itself arrives through the drone's eye. The README frames the contribution as a "Belief-Coupled" UGV that proactively slows and steers into the FoV, paired with an adaptive Tube-MPC that climbs during high-curvature segments to widen the visual field. (Several README claims about the toolchain and result paths are out of date; see §8.)

2 · Relationship to ECC 2024 — the UAV-planning delta

The headline extension. In Ferreira Santos et al. (ECC 2024) the UAV follows fixed, pre-defined waypoints at a fixed altitude — there is no aerial planning, only tracking of known points. This codebase adds online Tube MPC for the UAV itself: a nominal planner (uav_mpc_node.py) that optimizes a 3-D velocity trajectory — including vertical velocity, so altitude is a genuine decision variable — and an ancillary tube controller (uav_ancillary_node.py) that robustly tracks the nominal plan under disturbance and re-solves it on four event triggers. The aerial side is no longer a passive carrier of fixed waypoints; it is a closed-loop, disturbance-rejecting, observability-seeking planner.

2.2 · What the UAV planner optimizes uav_mpc_node.py

Decision variable is the velocity sequence $\mathbf{u}\in\mathbb{R}^{10\times3}$. State is propagated as a point-mass single integrator, $$\mathbf{p}_k=\mathbf{p}_0+\Big(\textstyle\sum_{j\le k}\mathbf{u}_j\Big)\,dt.$$ The cost is a horizon-weighted sum (weights $\mathbf{w}=\text{linspace}(1,2,N)$, so later steps matter more):

$$J=\sum_k w_k\|\mathbf{p}_k-\mathbf{g}\|^2 \;+\; w_z\sum_k w_k(z_k-z_\text{opt})^2 \;+\; w_\text{alt}\sum_k w_k\frac{\|\mathbf{p}^{xy}_k-\mathbf{g}^{xy}\|^2}{z_k^2+0.1} \;+\; 0.1\sum_k\|\mathbf{u}_k\|^2$$

Term 1 tracks the UGV's predicted position $\mathbf g$ at the end of its horizon (read from /ugv_predicted_traj), so the drone flies toward where the ground robot is going, not where it is. Note this is a full 3-vector norm and $\mathbf g$ carries $z\approx0$, so Term 1 also exerts a downward pull on altitude that Term 2 must counteract. Term 2 ($w_z=3$, a plain quadratic weight — the code's sigma_z parameter, not a standard deviation) is the altitude regularizer that pulls the drone toward the precision-optimal height $z_\text{opt}$. Term 3 ($w_\text{alt}=50$) is the key visibility heuristic: lateral offset is divided by altitude squared, encoding that a target far off the optical axis is tolerable only if the drone is high enough for it to remain in frame — it couples horizontal centering and altitude in one term. Term 4 is a small control-effort penalty for smoothness. Bounds: $v_x,v_y\in[-1.5,1.5]$, $v_z\in[-v_{z,\max},v_{z,\max}]$ with $v_{z,\max}=1$; SLSQP, maxiter=15, warm-started from the previous solution.

Altitude is a decision variable, not a heuristic bump. The only place altitude is "frozen" is the legacy use_timer=true path, which forces $v_z\in[0,0]$. In the default event-triggered mode the optimizer freely chooses vertical velocity, trading off the $z_\text{opt}$ regularizer against the visibility term. The README's phrasing that the ancillary node "triggers altitude increases based on curvature" is imprecise: curvature fires a replan request, and the planner's cost then decides altitude.

2.3 · When the event trigger fires uav_ancillary_node.py

The ancillary node runs at 20 Hz and publishes a Bool on /uav_replan_trigger (which calls uav_mpc_node.solve_mpc) under four prioritized conditions:

  • Level 1a — Tube Exit (critical, no lockout): fires when $e_k^\top P_0 e_k > \alpha_k^2$, i.e. the actual UAV state has left the invariant tube around the nominal plan.
  • Level 1b — ArUco Spike (1 s lockout): fires when $\operatorname{tr}\Sigma_{xy}>0.12$ and the current trace exceeds $1.5\times$ its value ~0.5 s earlier — a sudden estimation-quality collapse, e.g. the marker about to leave frame.
  • Level 2 — Curvature Anticipation (1 s lockout): estimates path curvature $\kappa=\Delta\theta/\Delta s$ over the first two segments of the UGV's predicted trajectory; fires if $\max\kappa>0.4$. This is the "see the turn coming, replan to widen FoV" trigger.
  • Level 3 — Drift Maintenance (1 s lockout): fires if $\operatorname{tr}\Sigma_{xy}>0.07$ and more than $T_\text{min}=3$ s have elapsed since the last replan — a slow-drift safety net.

2.4 · The tube / invariant set

The ancillary control law is the textbook ancillary-MPC form $$u_\text{anc}=\bar u-K\,e_k,\qquad e_k=\mathbf{x}_\text{uav}-\bar{\mathbf{x}},$$ where $(\bar{\mathbf x},\bar u)$ are interpolated from the nominal plan. Membership in the tube is the ellipsoidal test $e_k^\top P_0 e_k\le\alpha_k^2$, and the tube radius adapts to estimation quality: $$\alpha_k=\alpha_\text{min}+(\alpha_\text{max}-\alpha_\text{min})\cdot\operatorname{clip}\!\Big(\tfrac{\operatorname{tr}\Sigma_{xy}-\text{tr}_\text{min}}{\text{tr}_\text{max}-\text{tr}_\text{min}},0,1\Big).$$

$P_0$ (the shape of the invariant set) and the feedback gain $K$ are not computed in this repo — they are loaded from a precomputed file tube_params.npz. The RPI-set / constraint-tightening derivation lives in an upstream "Phase 1" that is not part of these sources (see §8). When the file is absent the node falls back to $K=2I_3$ and $P_0=6.86\,I_3$. The adaptive $\alpha_k$ is genuinely novel relative to ECC: the robust tube breathes with the EKF covariance, tightening when the estimate is sharp and loosening when perception degrades.

3.1 · One camera frame, end to end

(a) Image → perception. Gazebo's gz-sim-sensors-system renders the downward camera and publishes gz.msgs.Image on /drone/camera/image. The ros_gz_bridge converts it and remaps it to /overhead_camera/image (sensor_msgs/Image). middle_path_follower receives it, converts via cv_bridge to a BGR array, and on the first frame computes (once) a pixel→metre scale $s=\frac{2\,h\tan(\text{fov}/2)}{W}$ with a hard-coded camera height $h=5$ m and FoV $1.047$ rad. Because $h$ is fixed, the scale — and the world coordinates derived from it — is only correct at altitude $z=5$ m; since the UAV planner is free to choose altitude, the metric conversion is off by a factor $z/5$ whenever $z\neq5$.

(b) Perception → two products. It runs cv2.aruco.detectMarkers (dictionary DICT_6X6_250); for marker ID 55 it takes the corner centroid as the pixel position and the top-edge midpoint to derive heading. That pixel pose is converted to world metres — $x=(c_x-W/2)\,s$, $y=(c_y-H/2)\,s$ — and published as /aruco_pose (PoseStamped, world frame). Independently it thresholds the grayscale image, flood-fills the lane region from a seed projected ahead of the robot, runs a distance transform to find the lane ridge (centerline), then within a 60-px circle around a 50-px lookahead point takes the argmax of the distance map as the target. The target is rotated into the robot body frame and published as /waypoints (PoseStamped, base_link: $x$ forward, $y$ left).

(c) Perception → estimation. ekf_node runs a 20 Hz prediction timer using the last /cmd_vel. It corrects with two asynchronous measurements: /odom (gives $v,\omega$, $2\times5$ Jacobian) and /aruco_pose (pose $x,y,\theta$ padded with the current velocity estimate into a full-state pseudo-measurement $z=[x,y,\theta,\mu_v,\mu_\omega]$, so $H=I_5$). On the ArUco update the measurement-noise matrix $R_k$ is scaled inversely with view quality $q_k$, which is itself computed from the current /uav_gps_pose and the measured UGV position. It publishes /belief_state ($\mu$ as a pose, $\Sigma$ packed into a $6\times6$ covariance).

(d) Estimation → UGV control. belief_mpc_node, triggered on each /waypoints message, optimizes a 5-step control sequence with SLSQP. Inside the cost it propagates both the mean and the covariance, applies a smooth FoV-gated covariance update, and adds $\lambda\operatorname{tr}\Sigma$ plus an explicit FoV-exit penalty to the usual tracking terms. It emits the first control on /cmd_vel (TwistStamped) and the full predicted rollout on /ugv_predicted_traj (Path, world frame).

(e) UGV control → actuation (loop closes). /cmd_vel crosses the TurtleBot3 ros_gz_bridge into Gazebo's diff-drive; the robot moves, Gazebo emits fresh /odom, and the camera sees the new pose on the next frame — closing the ground loop.

(f) UAV branch (parallel). uav_mpc_node consumes /ugv_predicted_traj, /belief_state and /uav_gps_pose; when poked on /uav_replan_trigger it solves the 3-D planner above and publishes /uav_nominal_traj (Path). uav_ancillary_node interpolates that nominal plan at 20 Hz, computes the tracking error $e_k$, applies $u=\bar u-Ke$, and publishes /model/uav/cmd_vel (Twist). That command crosses the bridge into the drone's VelocityControl plugin; the drone moves and re-publishes /model/uav/pose (bridged to /uav_gps_pose) — closing the aerial loop and feeding back into the EKF's $R_k$ and the belief MPC's FoV terms. The ancillary node also evaluates the four event triggers and, when one fires, requests a fresh nominal plan.

6 · System requirements

  • GPU / CUDA: No torch/tensorflow/cupy/CUDA usage anywhere in the code (the pinned torch is the CPU build and is never imported). All optimization is CPU-bound SLSQP via SciPy.
  • Graphics acceleration: Required for the simulator. The Gazebo world declares the sensors system with <render_engine>ogre2</render_engine>; rendering the downward camera needs hardware-accelerated OpenGL. This is a Gazebo/GPU requirement, not a compute (CUDA) one.
  • Gazebo: Harmonic (matching ros-gz-* 1.0.22). Active camera sensor is 640×480 @ 20 Hz; an alternate 1920×1080 @ 30 Hz overhead camera exists but is commented out in the world.
  • RAM: Not specified in code. No large buffers are allocated — the only history is tr_sigma_history capped at ~20 samples, and the logger accumulates per-run arrays at 10 Hz. Memory footprint is dominated by Gazebo, not the nodes.
  • ROS 2 transport: Default DDS / rmw; all pub/sub use the default QoS with depth 10. No transport assumptions beyond ROS 2 defaults are encoded.
  • Real-time budget: The code targets a 150 ms control budget (comment in uav_mpc_node; README cites ~16 ms achieved). UAV MPC uses maxiter=15, both UGV MPCs maxiter=20.
  • Filesystem: The ancillary node reads tube parameters from a hard-coded absolute path on the original author's machine (/home/shlok-mehndiratta/.gemini/antigravity/scratch/tube_params.npz). On any other host it silently uses fallback gains — see §8. Logs are written to ~/experiment_logs/.

7 · Per-node walkthrough

7A · UGV-side & shared nodes

middle_path_follower perception

Role: Turns each drone camera frame into a body-frame waypoint for the UGV and a world-frame ArUco pose for the EKF.

Subscribes:
  • overhead_camera/image — sensor_msgs/Image
Publishes:
  • /waypoints — PoseStamped (body frame)
  • /aruco_pose — PoseStamped (world frame)
  • /nearest_point — PoseStamped; /middle_path/debug_image — Image

Key math

Pixel→metre scale, assuming pinhole camera at fixed height $h$:

$$s=\frac{2\,h\tan(\text{fov}/2)}{W},\quad h=5,\ \text{fov}=1.047.$$

This converts marker pixel coordinates to world metres, but only while the drone sits at $h=5$ m: the height is hard-coded and $s$ is computed once on the first frame, so the conversion is inconsistent with the UAV's altitude being a free decision variable. The waypoint is then the lane-centerline maximum of a distance transform within a circular mask placed a 50-px lookahead ahead of the robot heading, rotated into the body frame as $(\,d\cos\phi,\ d\sin\phi\,)$ with $d$ in metres and $\phi$ the relative bearing. The centerline itself is the ridge of cv2.distanceTransform over the flood-filled lane region, so the target is always the most "central" reachable lane pixel.

ekf_node estimation

Role: 5-state EKF that fuses ArUco pose and wheel odometry into the belief $(\mu,\Sigma)$ that both controllers consume.

Subscribes:
  • /cmd_vel — TwistStamped (control input for prediction)
  • /odom — nav_msgs/Odometry ($v,\omega$ measurement)
  • /aruco_pose — PoseStamped ($x,y,\theta$ measurement)
  • /uav_gps_pose — Pose (for dynamic $R_k$)
Publishes:
  • /belief_state — PoseWithCovarianceStamped

Key math — state & process model (ugv_model.py)

State $\mathbf{x}=[x,y,\theta,v,\omega]^\top$. Unicycle kinematics with first-order actuator lag (trapezoidal velocity), $\alpha_v=2$ ($\tau=0.5$s), $\alpha_\omega=5$ ($\tau=0.2$s):

$$\begin{aligned} v_{k+1} &= v_k + \alpha_v(v_\text{cmd} - v_k)\,dt \\ \omega_{k+1} &= \omega_k + \alpha_\omega(\omega_\text{cmd} - \omega_k)\,dt \\ x_{k+1} &= x_k + \frac{\bar v}{\bar\omega}\big(\sin(\theta + \bar\omega\,dt) - \sin\theta\big) \\ y_{k+1} &= y_k - \frac{\bar v}{\bar\omega}\big(\cos(\theta + \bar\omega\,dt) - \cos\theta\big) \end{aligned}$$

$\bar v,\bar\omega$ are the trapezoidal averages of current and next velocity; the closed-form arc integral (used when $|\bar\omega|\ge10^{-4}$, else a straight-line limit) is exact for constant-curvature motion and avoids Euler drift. The actuator-lag states mean the filter knows the robot cannot instantaneously reach a commanded velocity — important because the same model is reused inside the belief MPC.

Key math — Jacobian

$$F=I_5+\begin{bmatrix}\cdot&\cdot&-v\sin\theta\,dt&\cos\theta\,dt&\cdot\\ \cdot&\cdot&v\cos\theta\,dt&\sin\theta\,dt&\cdot\\ \cdot&\cdot&\cdot&\cdot&dt\\ \cdot&\cdot&\cdot&-\alpha_v dt&\cdot\\ \cdot&\cdot&\cdot&\cdot&-\alpha_\omega dt\end{bmatrix}$$

$F$ is the first-order (forward-Euler) Jacobian used to propagate the covariance, $\Sigma=F\Sigma F^\top+Q$ — note it is not the exact derivative of the arc-integral model printed above (e.g. it drops $\partial x/\partial\omega$ and the trapezoidal-average/lag factors in the $v,\theta$ partials); the code deliberately uses this cheaper Euler Jacobian. Here $Q=\text{diag}(10^{-4},10^{-4},4{\times}10^{-4},10^{-4},2.5{\times}10^{-3})$. The bottom two rows encode that velocity uncertainty decays toward the commanded value at the actuator rate.

Key math — Joseph-form update & dynamic noise

$$K=\Sigma H^\top S^{-1},\ \Sigma^+=(I-KH)\Sigma(I-KH)^\top+KRK^\top,\qquad R_k=\frac{R_\text{full}}{q_k+10^{-3}}$$

The Joseph form keeps $\Sigma$ symmetric positive-definite even with marginal numerics. Odometry uses $H=[\,0\,0\,0\,1\,0;\,0\,0\,0\,0\,1\,]$ with $R_\text{odom}=\text{diag}(4{\times}10^{-4},2.5{\times}10^{-3})$; ArUco uses $H=I_5$. The crucial coupling is $R_k$: measurement noise is divided by the view quality $q_k\in(0,1)$, so when the drone has a poor view the ArUco measurement is trusted far less. (A blocking sanity-check parameter can drop ArUco between two timestamps to test dropout robustness.)

ugv_model.py / view_quality.py libraries

Role: Shared math used by the EKF and both belief-coupled controllers. No ROS node.

Key math — view quality $q(x,y,z)$

$$r_\text{fov}=z\tan\tfrac{\theta_\text{fov}}{2},\quad q=\underbrace{\frac{1}{1+e^{\beta(d-r_\text{fov})}}}_{\text{FoV boundary}}\cdot\underbrace{e^{-d^2/(2\sigma_{xy}^2)}}_{\text{centering}}\cdot\underbrace{e^{-(z-z_\text{opt})^2/(2\sigma_z^2)}}_{\text{altitude precision}}$$

$d$ is the horizontal UAV→UGV distance. The first factor is a smooth (differentiable) gate that goes 1→0 as the target crosses the FoV radius; the second rewards keeping the target near the optical axis ($\sigma_{xy}=r_\text{fov}/2$); the third is a Gaussian preferring the precision-optimal altitude $z_\text{opt}=5$, $\sigma_z=3$. The product is the single scalar that feeds the EKF's $R_k$ and the belief MPC's covariance gating — being differentiable, it is safe inside gradient-based SLSQP. A finite-difference gradient helper is also provided.

belief_mpc_node UGV control core contribution

Role: Information-aware UGV NMPC — follows the lane while actively keeping itself observable to the drone.

Subscribes:
  • /waypoints — PoseStamped
  • /belief_state — PoseWithCovarianceStamped
  • /uav_gps_pose — Pose
Publishes:
  • /cmd_vel — TwistStamped
  • /ugv_predicted_traj — nav_msgs/Path (drives the UAV planner)

Key math — cost (horizon $N=5$, $dt=0.2$)

$$J=\sum_{k}\Big[\,w_d\,\|\mathbf p_k-\mathbf p_\text{tgt}\|^2+w_h\,e_{\theta,k}^2+w_r\,\|\Delta\mathbf u_k\|^2+\lambda\operatorname{tr}\Sigma_k+2\,(\max(0,d_k-r_\text{fov}))^2\,\Big]$$

Weights: $w_d=10$ (distance to target), $w_h=10$ (heading error, switched off within $\approx0.22$ m of the target — the code gates on squared distance $<0.05\,\text{m}^2$), $w_r=5$ (control-rate smoothness), $\lambda=1$ (uncertainty), and a fixed $2.0$ on the FoV-exit penalty. The two belief-coupled terms are what make this "information-aware": $\lambda\operatorname{tr}\Sigma_k$ penalizes predicted estimation uncertainty (so the robot prefers maneuvers the drone can observe well), and the hinge penalty $(\max(0,d_k-r_\text{fov}))^2$ directly punishes leaving the field of view.

Key math — covariance gating inside the rollout

$$\gamma=\frac{1}{1+e^{\beta(d-r_\text{fov})}},\qquad \Sigma_k=(1-\gamma)\,\Sigma_\text{pred}+\gamma\,\Sigma_\text{obs}$$

For each predicted step the controller re-derives whether the robot would be visible ($\gamma\in[0,1]$, sharpness $\beta=5$) and blends the no-observation prediction $\Sigma_\text{pred}=F\Sigma F^\top+Q$ with the post-measurement $\Sigma_\text{obs}$ (a Joseph update with the same dynamic $R_k$). This is the predictive heart of the method: the MPC simulates how its own future motion will change the quality of the drone's estimate, then optimizes against that. Solved with SLSQP, bounds $v\in[0,0.22]$, $\omega\in[-2,2]$, maxiter=20.

mpc_node / stanley_node / pure_pursuit_node baselines

Role: Alternative UGV controllers selectable via the controller:= launch arg, used as comparison baselines against belief_mpc. All subscribe /waypoints and publish /cmd_vel (TwistStamped); none use the belief state.

  • mpc_node — same unicycle+lag model and SLSQP setup as the belief MPC but a plain tracking cost: $w_d\|\cdot\|^2+w_h e_\theta^2+w_r\|\Delta u\|^2$ ($w_d=10,w_h=2,w_r=5$), no covariance or FoV term.
  • stanley_node — Stanley steering $\delta=\psi_e+\arctan\!\frac{k\,\text{cte}}{1+v}$ with cross-track error $\text{cte}=y\cos\psi-x\sin\psi$, $k=2$, then $\omega=v\tan\delta/0.2$.
  • pure_pursuit_node — curvature $\kappa=2y/d^2$, $\omega=v\kappa$, with a speed halving when $|\kappa|>1$.

uav_mpc_node UAV nominal planner new vs ECC

Role: Online 3-D trajectory planner for the drone — the outer (nominal) loop of the Tube MPC. Replaces ECC's fixed waypoints.

Subscribes:

  • /belief_state — PoseWithCovarianceStamped
  • /ugv_predicted_traj — Path (the UGV's MPC rollout)
  • /uav_replan_trigger — std_msgs/Bool (event-triggered solve)
Publishes:
  • /uav_nominal_traj — Path ($N{+}1$ poses)

Key math

See §2.2 for the full cost. In short: point-mass single-integrator dynamics, $N=10$, $dt=0.2$, decision $\mathbf u_k=[v_x,v_y,v_z]$, cost = goal tracking + altitude regularizer ($w_z=3$, the sigma_z weight, toward $z_\text{opt}$) + visibility heuristic ($w_\text{alt}=50$, lateral offset ÷ altitude²) + control effort. The target $\mathbf g$ is the UGV's predicted position at the end of its horizon, not its current position. SLSQP, maxiter=15, warm-started by rolling the previous solution. By default runs only on the replan trigger (no periodic timer); the legacy use_timer=true mode adds a 1 Hz solve and freezes altitude.

uav_ancillary_node UAV Tube-MPC tracker new vs ECC

Role: The inner (ancillary) loop — robustly tracks the nominal plan at 20 Hz, keeps the true state inside an adaptive invariant tube, and fires the four replan triggers. Also runs the takeoff state machine.

Subscribes:
  • /model/uav/pose — Pose (actual UAV state)
  • /belief_state — PoseWithCovarianceStamped (for adaptive tube + spike/drift triggers)
  • /ugv_predicted_traj — Path (for curvature trigger)
  • /uav_nominal_traj — Path (the plan to track)
Publishes:
  • /model/uav/cmd_vel — geometry_msgs/Twist (drone velocity command)
  • /uav_replan_trigger — Bool

Key math — ancillary law & adaptive tube

$$u_\text{anc}=\bar u-K\,e_k,\qquad \text{inside tube}\iff e_k^\top P_0 e_k\le\alpha_k^2,$$ $$\alpha_k=\alpha_\text{min}+(\alpha_\text{max}-\alpha_\text{min})\operatorname{clip}\!\Big(\tfrac{\operatorname{tr}\Sigma_{xy}-\text{tr}_\text{min}}{\text{tr}_\text{max}-\text{tr}_\text{min}},0,1\Big)$$

$e_k$ is the error between the actual UAV pose and the time-interpolated nominal state. The feedback $-Ke_k$ pulls the drone back onto the plan; the ellipsoid $e^\top P_0 e\le\alpha_k^2$ defines the robust positively-invariant tube the state must stay within. The novelty is the adaptive radius: $\alpha_k$ grows with the EKF position-covariance trace ($\alpha\in[0.5,2]$ as $\operatorname{tr}\Sigma_{xy}$ sweeps $[0.005,0.15]$), so the tube relaxes precisely when perception is poor and tightens when the estimate is sharp. $K$ and $P_0$ are loaded from a precomputed .npz (fallback $K=2I$, $P_0=6.86I$).

Event triggers

Four prioritized conditions publish a replan request — detailed in §2.3: 1a tube exit (critical), 1b ArUco covariance spike, 2 curvature anticipation ($\kappa=\Delta\theta/\Delta s>0.4$), 3 drift maintenance ($>T_\text{min}=3$s since last solve). Levels 1b/2/3 each have a 1 s lockout. A TAKEOFF→TRACKING state machine climbs to 5 m at 0.5 m/s before tracking begins, with a one-shot bootstrap trigger to request the first plan.

8 · Open questions / gaps

These are points where the code is unclear, unfinished, or inconsistent with the README. They are worth resolving before the work is built on further.

1 · Tube parameters are not in the repo and use a hard-coded private path. uav_ancillary_node loads $K$ and $P_0$ from /home/shlok-mehndiratta/.gemini/antigravity/scratch/tube_params.npz. On any other machine that file is missing and the node silently falls back to $K=2I_3$, $P_0=6.86I_3$ — so unless that file is reproduced, every external run uses placeholder gains, not the designed RPI set. The RPI-set / constraint-tightening derivation ("Phase 1") is referenced but absent from these sources.
2 · Nominal-trajectory timestep mismatch (likely bug). uav_mpc_node emits the plan at $dt=0.2$ s, but uav_ancillary_node.get_nominal_state interpolates assuming $dt=0.3$ s steps. The interpolated nominal position, and especially the finite-difference $\bar u$ it derives, will be scaled/indexed incorrectly — the tube tracker is following a time-distorted version of the plan.
3 · Altitude position limits are declared but never enforced. uav_mpc_node reads z_min=3, z_max=10 but never uses them as constraints; altitude is bounded only indirectly through the velocity bound $v_z\in[-1,1]$ and the soft $z_\text{opt}$ cost. There is no hard floor/ceiling on commanded altitude.
4 · Inconsistent z_opt. uav_mpc_node default is $z_\text{opt}=5.0$, the launch file passes $6.0$, and view_quality.py hard-codes $5.0$. The planner and the view-quality metric driving the EKF/belief MPC can therefore disagree on the "optimal" altitude.
5 · Dead / unused parameters and wiring. force_fallback is declared in the launch file and set as a node parameter but never read inside uav_mpc_node. uav_mpc_node remaps /uav_cmd_vel→/model/uav/cmd_vel yet never publishes a cmd_vel (only the nominal path) — the ancillary node is the sole driver of the drone. Pinned deps torch and sympy are never imported.
6 · README is partly stale. It claims ROS 2 "Humble or Iron" (actual: Jazzy), references a mission.launch.py entry point (actual: middle_path_follower.launch.py), and says telemetry is written to ugv_uav_control/data (actual: ~/experiment_logs/). The performance table (e.g. 0.463 m mean error, 87 % visibility) is attributed to "Phase 1/Phase 3" artifacts not contained in this package.
7 · Minor: redundant publish & self-referential ArUco rows. belief_mpc_node.waypoint_callback publishes /cmd_vel twice per solve (once in the success branch, once at the end). The ArUco EKF update uses $H=I_5$ with the velocity entries of $z$ set to the current estimate $\mu_{3,4}$, so those two rows carry no new information yet still contribute through $R$.
